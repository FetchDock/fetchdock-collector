# FetchDock report collector privacy policy

**Version:** 1 · **Last updated:** 25 November 2026

FetchDock is open-source software that people install and run on their own servers ("instances"). Instances are run by their own operators, not by us. This policy only covers the **report collector** at reports.fetchdock.dev, which receives failure reports that users choose to share with the FetchDock developers. How an instance handles your data is described in that instance's own privacy policy.

## 1. Who is responsible
PBX_g33k, the maintainer of FetchDock, contact privacy@fetchdock.dev ("we").

## 2. How a report reaches us
A report reaches us only if all of these are true:
1. the operator of your instance turned on sharing with the FetchDock developers and accepted this policy;
2. you ticked "Also share with FetchDock developers" on that report, after the extension showed you its exact contents and a link to this policy;
3. if your instance's operator chose to review reports before they are forwarded: the operator forwarded it.

We do not receive anything from instances that have not enabled sharing, and nothing about reports you did not choose to share.

## 3. What we receive

| Data | Notes |
|---|---|
| The URL of the page or media, its host and the site FetchDock matched | URLs can identify you if they contain names, account ids or private links; the extension offers to remove the query string before sending |
| Your comment | Free text you wrote; please don't include personal details |
| Optional, only if you ticked them: your browser's user agent, the referring page (by default only its site address), and technical details (downloader, its version, the error message, the extension version) | Error messages are shown to you before sending |
| A comment from your instance's administrator, if they add one | The administrator writes it; it may be added after the report was sent |
| The identifier we issued to the instance when its operator enabled sharing, the instance's FetchDock version, the policy version you agreed to, and timestamps | The instance identifier is not linked to any person; it lets us group reports, limit abuse and act on deletion requests |
| The IP address of the instance's server | Received when the instance connects. Your own IP address is not sent; the instance forwards the report |

We do **not** receive your cookies, passwords, account identifiers on the instance, your downloads, or which instance user sent the report.

## 4. Why we use it
To find out which websites and media fail, prioritise support for them, and fix bugs in FetchDock. We do not sell report data, use it for advertising, or try to identify the people behind reports.

## 5. Legal basis (GDPR)
- Reports and their contents: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a), given per report. You can withdraw it at any time (section 7).
- Administrator comments and the instance's IP address and identifier: our legitimate interest in maintaining FetchDock and protecting the collector against abuse, Art. 6(1)(f).

## 6. Storage and retention
Reports are stored on servers we run ourselves; no hosting provider has access to them. Connections to the collector pass through Cloudflare, which protects it against attacks and, to do so, processes the connection (including the instance's IP address and the request) on our behalf. We keep reports for 24 months after they were received, or until the issue they describe is resolved, whichever is later. Connection logs with IP addresses are kept for 30 days. Access is limited to the FetchDock maintainers.

## 7. Withdrawing a report and your rights
- Withdrawing a report in the extension asks your instance to delete our copy as well; we delete it within 30 days.
- If your instance can no longer do that, contact us at privacy@fetchdock.dev with the report identifier shown in the extension under "My reports".
- You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we can't tell who sent a report, we need the report identifier to find your data.

## 8. Changes
We publish each version of this policy at https://reports.fetchdock.dev/policy with its version number (older versions at /policy/{version}). If a change affects how we use reports, we treat earlier consents as no longer valid: instances then stop forwarding reports agreed under the old version until you or their operator agree again.
